The development of the **BKI SKARIGA Recruitment Information System** is the result of the dedication, collaboration, and commitment of every member of the development team. This project was built to support the digitalization of vocational job placement and alumni career tracking at SMK PGRI 3 Malang in an integrated and reliable way.

## Commitment & System Development

During the design process, the team integrated a *decoupled* architecture comprising a high-performance backend API, a responsive user interface built to modern accessibility standards, and a comprehensive recruitment business flow.

The system was completed successfully through intensive collaboration, thorough testing at every functional layer, and alignment of requirements among the school, industry partners, and students.
