---
title: Job Vacancy Publishing & Application Submission
description: The process from HRD creating a vacancy to students/alumni submitting applications
---

## 1. Job Vacancy Creation by HRD

Partner companies can post job vacancies independently through the HRD portal:

- **Vacancy Attributes:** Title, position, intake quota, registration deadline, work placement location, employment type (full-time, contract, internship), and qualifications.
- **Applicant Target:**
  - `class_12_only`: Exclusively for active final-year students.
  - `alumni_only`: Exclusively for graduates / alumni.
  - `all`: Open to both grade 12 students and alumni.
- **Major Filter:** Restriction to relevant vocational majors (e.g., Software Engineering, Light Vehicle Engineering, etc.).

## 2. Automatic Notifications

When a vacancy has the published status (`published`):
- The system runs `JobVacancyService::notifyTargetApplicants()`.
- It sends a notification email (`JobVacancyNotificationMail`) to the inbox of users who meet the qualifications.
- It emits a real-time in-app notification over the Laravel Reverb WebSocket.

## 3. Application Submission by Students/Alumni

Students and alumni can browse open vacancies in the student portal:
- The system validates that the applicant's major and role match the vacancy criteria.
- The applicant ensures their profile data and E-Portfolio documents (CV, competency certificates, award certificates, academic transcript) are complete.
- Submits the application with an initial `pending` status (awaiting document verification).
