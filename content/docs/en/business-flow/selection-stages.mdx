---
title: Document Screening, Tests, & Result Evaluation
description: The document review flow, test scheduling, and selection result announcements
---

## 1. Administrative Document Screening

HRD reviews incoming applicants through the **Applicant Review** module:
- HRD views the complete profile, education history, and PDF portfolio documents.
- **Decision `lolos`:** The application status moves to `in_progress`. The applicant is eligible for the follow-up test stage.
- **Decision `tidak_lolos`:** The application status changes to `rejected`. The system automatically sends the applicant an administrative screening rejection notification (`admin_review_failed`) along with the evaluation notes.

## 2. Test Scheduling & Reminders

For applicants who pass document screening:
- HRD creates the test agenda (psychotest, written test, vocational practical test, or interview).
- Sets the date, time, venue, and minimum passing score.
- The system sends the schedule notification (`test_schedule`) to all participants.
- The **Remind All / Remind Participant** feature lets HRD trigger schedule reminder messages ahead of the test day.

## 3. Attendance Validation

- The BKI admin at the school manages the queue list of applicant attendance at the test venue.
- Staff validate participant attendance to ensure only those present are processed to the next evaluation stage.

## 4. Selection Result Evaluation & Final Decision

Once the tests and interviews are complete, HRD inputs the evaluation scores:
- Score components: Psychotest Score, Interview Score, MCU Score, and Final Score.
- The decision letter / placement letter can be uploaded as a PDF or image.
- **Selection Result Publication:**
  - `diterima`: The applicant receives a passing notification (`selection_passed`), can download the announcement letter, and the data is forwarded to the Job Placement module.
  - `tidak_diterima`: The applicant receives a final selection failure notification (`selection_failed`).
  - `cadangan`: Waitlist status in case a slot holder withdraws.
