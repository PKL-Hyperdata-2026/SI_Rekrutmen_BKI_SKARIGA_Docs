title: Job Placement & Tracer Study
description: Managing graduate job placement and alumni tracer surveys
---

## 1. Job Placement Module

Applicants that HRD marks as passed automatically enter the job placement records:

- **Recorded Data:** Student/alumni name, placement company, job position, acceptance date, and start date of work (`start_date`).
- **Job Retention Monitoring:** The BKI admin and HRD track the employment status of graduates at these checkpoints:
  - 3 months after starting work
  - 6 months after starting work
  - 12 months after starting work
- **Employment Retention Status:** Actively employed, contract extension, promotion to permanent employee, transfer, or resignation.

## 2. Alumni Tracer Study

The Tracer Study module tracks where graduates go — into employment, entrepreneurship, or further study at a higher education institution:

- **Self-Submission:** Alumni can complete the tracer study form themselves on the alumni portal, covering their current status (employed, studying, entrepreneur, or job seeking), institution/company name, starting income, and waiting time before employment.
- **Automatic Sync:** Graduates already recorded in the job placement module are automatically linked to tracer study data, so they never have to enter the same information twice.
- **Metrics & Analysis:** The BKI admin has a statistics dashboard for job absorption by major, average waiting time, and the geographic distribution of industry partners.
