The recruitment cycle involves four interconnected parties: **Students/Alumni**, **Partner Companies (HRD)**, **BKI Admin**, and the **Automated System**.

## Details of Each Stage

### 1. Post a Job Vacancy & Send Notifications
Partner companies enter job vacancy data through their HRD portal. Once a vacancy is set to the published status (`published`), the system automatically filters which students or alumni match the major. Notification emails and in-app alerts are sent straight to their accounts.

### 2. Registration & Document Review (Administration)
Interested applicants can apply directly through the student portal. The digital portfolio documents they have uploaded (such as a CV, competency test certificates, and internship certificates) are immediately accessible to HRD.
- If the documents meet the requirements, HRD advances the applicant to the next stage.
- If they do not, the status is changed to rejected and the system automatically sends a notification that the applicant did not pass the administrative selection.

### 3. Test Schedule & Attendance Validation
Applicants who pass the document selection are added to a test schedule (psychometric test, vocational test, or interview). HRD sets the date and location of the test, then sends reminders to the participants. At the school, the BKI Admin is responsible for checking and validating the attendance of participants who show up.

### 4. Final Evaluation & Job Placement
HRD enters the final scores and uploads the decision letter. Applicants who are accepted receive a passing notification and can immediately download their placement letter from the portal.

### 5. Employment Monitoring & Tracer Study
Graduates who are already employed have their retention status monitored at the 3rd, 6th, and 12th months. This data feeds directly into BKI's job absorption reports and the alumni tracer study questionnaire.
