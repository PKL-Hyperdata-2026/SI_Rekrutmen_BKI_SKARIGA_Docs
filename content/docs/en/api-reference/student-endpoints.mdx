---
title: Student & Alumni Endpoints
description: Endpoint specification for the active student and alumni modules
---

The following endpoints are accessed by users with the `siswa` or `alumni` role.

## Self-Service Profile & E-Portfolio (`/api/siswa/portfolio` & `/api/alumni/portfolio`)

- `GET /api/siswa/portfolio/profile`: Retrieve academic profile data, personal details, and contact information.
- `GET /api/siswa/portfolio/options`: Dropdown options for portfolio categories, skills, and status.
- `PUT /api/siswa/portfolio/profile`: Update personal details and social media.
- `POST /api/siswa/portfolio/upload`: Upload portfolio documents (certificates, awards, CV) in PDF/image format, max 10MB.
- `DELETE /api/siswa/portfolio/{id}`: Delete a portfolio document.

*(Alumni can access the equivalent routes at `/api/alumni/portfolio/*`)*

## Job Vacancy Browsing & Applications (`/api/job-vacancies` & `/api/my-applications`)

- `GET /api/job-vacancies`: List active job vacancies open to the applicant's major and status.
- `GET /api/job-vacancies/options`: Filter options for company, job type, and work location.
- `GET /api/job-vacancies/{id}`: Job vacancy details, qualifications, and benefits.
- `POST /api/job-vacancies/{id}/apply`: Submit an application for the given job vacancy (`ApplyJobVacancyRequest`).
- `GET /api/my-applications`: List of all submitted applications with their current stage status.
- `GET /api/my-applications/{id}`: Application details, selection stage progress timeline, and a link to the acceptance letter if the applicant passed.

## Alumni Tracer Study (`/api/alumni/tracer-study`)

- `GET /api/alumni/tracer-study`: Retrieve the history of submitted graduate tracer survey data.
- `POST /api/alumni/tracer-study`: Create or update tracer study data (employment status, institution/company, starting salary, waiting period).
