---
title: BKI & Superadmin Admin Endpoints
description: Endpoint specifications for the BKI Administrator and Superadmin modules
---

The following endpoints are accessed by accounts with the `admin` or `superadmin` role.

## School & Partner Company Master Data

- `/api/admin/students`: CRUD for grade 12 active student master data.
- `/api/admin/alumni`: CRUD for alumni master data (including the feature to upgrade a student to alumni).
- `/api/admin/companies`: CRUD for partner company data and an active status toggle.
- `/api/admin/departments`: CRUD for vocational department data.
- `/api/admin/majors`: CRUD for competency / major data.

## Selection & Attendance Monitoring

- `GET /api/admin/recruitment-selections`: View-only recap of selection progress across all job vacancies.
- `GET /api/admin/attendances/queue`: Queue of attendance records for selection test participants awaiting validation by BKI staff.
- `GET /api/admin/attendances/history`: Attendance history for participants that have been validated.
- `PATCH /api/admin/attendances/{id}/validate`: Validate attendance for a single participant.
- `PATCH /api/admin/attendances/bulk-validate`: Bulk attendance validation.

## Report Recapitulation (`/api/admin/reports`)

- `GET /api/admin/reports/options`: Filter options for academic year, major, and date range.
- `GET /api/admin/reports/recruitment`: Statistical report on recruitment and pass rate per company.
- `GET /api/admin/reports/attendance`: Report on selection participant attendance rates.
- `GET /api/admin/reports/absorption`: Report on job absorption for grade 12 students and alumni.
- `GET /api/admin/reports/tracer-study`: Tracer study report, waiting period, and distribution of work locations.

## User Management (`/api/admin/users`) *(Superadmin Only)*

- `GET /api/admin/users`: List of user accounts (students, alumni, HRD, admin).
- `POST /api/admin/users`: Create a new account.
- `PUT /api/admin/users/{id}`: Update account information.
- `PATCH /api/admin/users/{id}/toggle-active`: Activate / deactivate a user account.
- `POST /api/admin/users/{id}/reset-password`: Manual password reset by a Superadmin.
- `DELETE /api/admin/users/{id}`: Delete a user account.
