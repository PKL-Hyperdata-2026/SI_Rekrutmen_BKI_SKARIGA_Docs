---
title: Company Endpoints (HRD)
description: Endpoint specification for the partner company HRD module
---

All of the following endpoints require an `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header, using an account with the `hrd` role that is linked to a company record (`company`).

## Job Vacancies (`/api/hrd/job-vacancies`)

- `GET /api/hrd/job-vacancies`: List of job vacancies belonging to the company (paginated + filters).
- `GET /api/hrd/job-vacancies/statistics`: Statistics on applicants, filled quotas, and active job vacancies.
- `GET /api/hrd/job-vacancies/options`: Dropdown options for categories, majors, and job types.
- `POST /api/hrd/job-vacancies`: Create a new job vacancy (`StoreHrdJobVacancyRequest`).
- `GET /api/hrd/job-vacancies/{id}`: Job vacancy details.
- `PUT /api/hrd/job-vacancies/{id}`: Update job vacancy data.
- `PATCH /api/hrd/job-vacancies/{id}/toggle-active`: Toggle a job vacancy between active and inactive.
- `DELETE /api/hrd/job-vacancies/{id}`: Delete a job vacancy (soft delete).

## Applicant Reviews (`/api/hrd/applicant-reviews`)

- `GET /api/hrd/applicant-reviews`: List of applicants who applied to the company's job vacancies (filter by job vacancy, review status, or search by name/NIS).
- `GET /api/hrd/applicant-reviews/options`: Filter options for job vacancy and status.
- `GET /api/hrd/applicant-reviews/{id}`: Applicant details, education history, and document portfolio.
- `PATCH /api/hrd/applicant-reviews/{id}/review`: Administrative selection decision (`decision: lolos | tidak_lolos`, `notes`).
- `POST /api/hrd/applicant-reviews/bulk-review`: Bulk administrative selection decisions (`application_ids: [...]`, `decision`, `notes`).

## Test Agenda & Schedules (`/api/hrd/test-schedules`)

- `GET /api/hrd/test-schedules`: List of the company's selection test agendas.
- `POST /api/hrd/test-schedules`: Create a new test schedule for applicants who passed the document selection.
- `GET /api/hrd/test-schedules/{id}`: Test schedule details.
- `GET /api/hrd/test-schedules/{id}/participants`: List of participants allocated to this test.
- `POST /api/hrd/test-schedules/{id}/remind-all`: Send a reminder notification to all test participants.
- `POST /api/hrd/test-schedules/{id}/participants/{participantId}/remind`: Send a reminder to a specific participant.

## Selection Results & Placement (`/api/hrd/selection-results` & `/api/hrd/job-placements`)

- `GET /api/hrd/selection-results`: List of score evaluations for selection test participants.
- `POST /api/hrd/selection-results/{applicationId}`: Save psychotest, interview, and MCU scores, the final score, notes, and the decision letter document (`StoreHrdSelectionResultRequest`).
- `PATCH /api/hrd/selection-results/{applicationId}/decision`: Change a participant's pass/fail decision (`UpdateHrdSelectionDecisionRequest`).
- `POST /api/hrd/selection-results/publish`: Publish the final selection results (`PublishHrdSelectionResultRequest`).
- `GET /api/hrd/job-placements`: List of students/alumni who have been placed in jobs at the company.
