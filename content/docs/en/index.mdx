The BKI SKARIGA Recruitment Information System helps SMK PGRI 3 Malang manage the entire job placement process for students and alumni in one central place.

The platform connects final-year students and alumni with industry partner companies that post job vacancies. The selection process is managed directly inside the application, from registration and portfolio document verification to test scheduling and monitoring graduates who have been hired.

## Who Uses This System?

1. **Grade 12 Students:** Search for internship or job vacancies, upload vocational certificate portfolios, and track selection status.
2. **Alumni:** Access job vacancies for graduates, apply for jobs, and fill in the career tracking questionnaire (tracer study).
3. **Companies (HRD):** Post job vacancies, review applicant portfolios, schedule selection tests, and announce final results.
4. **BKI Admin:** Manage master data for students, majors, and industry partners, validate test attendance at school, and monitor job absorption data.
5. **Superadmin:** Manage user accounts, access rights, and system settings across the board.
